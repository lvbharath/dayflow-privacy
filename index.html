Dayflow Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026 · Developer: SourceCodeBlackCrow

Dayflow is a tasks, calendar, countdown, reminders and notes app. It is free, has no ads, and is built so that your information stays on your device.

Information we collect

None. Dayflow has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party trackers, and it never sends the content you create anywhere. The app's only network access comes from the Google Play Billing Library, which Google provides for optional tips and which may send limited diagnostic information about billing to Google.

Information stored on your device

The tasks, lists, events, countdowns, reminders, notes and settings you create are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. Uninstalling the app or using “Erase All Data” deletes them.

Backups

If you have turned on Android backup for your Google account, Android may include Dayflow's data in your device backup. That backup is created, encrypted and stored by Google under your account, and is governed by Google's privacy policy. You can also export a backup file yourself; it is saved only to the location you choose.

Tips

Dayflow offers optional tips to support development. Tips are processed entirely by Google Play. We never receive your payment details; Google Play may share limited order information with us (such as the order number and the product purchased) as part of its standard developer reports.

Permissions

  • Notifications – to alert you about reminders, tasks, events and countdowns at the times you set.
  • Alarms & reminders (exact alarms) – so alerts arrive at exactly the right minute.
  • Run at startup – to restore your scheduled alerts after the phone restarts.
  • Vibration – for alert and haptic feedback.

Children

Dayflow does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
